# Solmon Jeong

**Robotics Engineer — Teleoperation, Haptics & Real-Time Control**

I am a Visiting Researcher at MIT's The 77 Lab, on assignment from Sumitomo Heavy Industries.

I develop robotic systems that connect human operation with physical interaction. My work spans microsurgical robots and construction machinery, combining mechanical design, sensing, real-time control, and experimental validation.

## Selected Projects

### Excavator Teleoperation with Force Feedback

A research platform integrating Unity simulation, real-time C++ control, and haptic interfaces to investigate how force feedback supports operation under visual delay and uncertainty.

### World-Space Haptic Interface

A custom Cartesian interface for task-space teleoperation, combining backdrivable mechanisms, passive gravity compensation, and bidirectional force feedback.

### Microsurgical Robotics

Leader–follower robotic systems combining custom mechanisms, force sensing, and real-time C++ control for precise manipulation and haptic interaction.

## Technical Focus

- Teleoperation and bilateral control
- Haptic interfaces and force feedback
- Real-time C++ and embedded communication
- Mechanical design and robot integration
- Simulation and experimental evaluation
